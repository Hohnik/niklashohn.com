How this page works

One WebGL shader draws the whole world twice per frame. The first pass writes the terrain height of every cell into a texture; the second asks, for each pixel, which layer lands on me? — searching from the top level down, because a fragment shader can colour itself but cannot move itself. The first hit wins, and that is the entire fake perspective. Press ~ to pull it apart.
