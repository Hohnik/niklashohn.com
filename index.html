How this page works

Three shader passes draw the world, all at cell resolution — the canvas is a quarter the width of the window and CSS scales it back up, so the pixel grid is real rather than rounded.

The first pass writes the terrain height of every cell into a texture. The second asks, for each pixel, which layer lands on me? — searching from the top level down, because a fragment shader can colour itself but cannot move itself. The first hit wins, and that is the entire fake perspective. The third does the colour, the contour ink, the relief shading and the plane — which is drawn as two triangles of half-planes rather than a sprite, so it turns to any angle for free.

Press ~ to pull it apart: five terrain generators, seven palettes, four contour styles, three lighting models, six plane shapes — twenty-three settings in all, and the six named presets are just bundles of them.
